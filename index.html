What you'll see when you connect

Gmail: Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app." That's expected for a private app shared between friends. Click Advanced, then Go to Recruiting Command Deck (unsafe), then allow read access. If Google says access is blocked, the person who sent you this link needs to add your Gmail address as a tester first.

Outlook / Hotmail: Microsoft will ask you to allow reading your mail. School or work accounts may show "Need admin approval", which means your IT department blocks outside mail apps; a personal Outlook or Hotmail account works without it.

More than one inbox: connect one here, then use + Gmail or + Outlook in the sidebar to add the rest. Every inbox is searched at once, a message that landed in two of them is counted once, and your notes are shared across all of them.

Yahoo, iCloud and others aren't supported. They don't offer a sign-in based way to read mail, and the alternative means handing your password to a server, which this app deliberately never does.

Recruiter contact log

Mail matched by recruiting keyword, by a known search-firm domain, or parsed from LinkedIn invites where the sender's title or company reads as recruiting. Names come from the sender header or their signature. Dismiss anything that slipped through.

Target list

Firms that run PE associate and VP on-cycle processes. Ones you're already in touch with are checked after your first scan. Check, rename, add or remove rows; it's saved on this device.

Firm debriefs

One dossier per search firm: what you asked to be put up for, how the conversation went, your next meeting, and the funds they're steering you toward. Funds already surfaced over email are detected automatically. Saves as you type.

Fund engagement ladder

Event threads scored by the strongest signal in the thread. Attended plus a real follow-up outranks attendance, which outranks a confirmed RSVP, a registration, and a cold invite. Threads about the same fund are merged.

The unfiltered view, in case the scanners missed something.
